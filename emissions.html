Greenhouse-gas accounting · January–October 2026

Gross emissions are not quantifiable

The evidence currently supports satellite screening indicators, not a reserve-wide estimate of greenhouse-gas emissions or net balance.

Unknown—not zero

All-gases, CO₂-only, and non-CO₂ gross-emissions quantities are blank because validated activity data, carbon-pool losses, and gas-specific emission factors are not available. Blank values are not zero-emission findings.

Required activity evidence is incomplete

No validated monthly inventory of disturbance, biomass loss, or combustion is available for the full reserve boundary.

Photosynthesis is not emissions

MODIS GPP/PSN are modelled plant-uptake indicators. They do not measure releases, all ecosystem pools, or net greenhouse-gas balance.

Fire data have limits

MODIS found no QA-valid burned pixels Jan–Aug; Sep–Oct products were unavailable in the queried catalog. This does not prove no fire occurred.

What would be needed to quantify emissions?

A defensible estimate requires validated disturbance or activity data; carbon stocks and pool-specific losses; suitable emission factors for CO₂, CH₄, and N₂O; temporal alignment; and uncertainty assessment. A fire-only model, vegetation index, or photosynthesis estimate cannot replace this evidence.
