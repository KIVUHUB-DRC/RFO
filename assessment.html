Assessment date · 4 November 2026

Forest & carbon assessment

Boundary-based synthesis of satellite indicators, data availability, and the evidence limits for estimating carbon stocks, emissions, and net balance.

Overall conclusion: The available evidence does not support a defensible estimate of the reserve’s 2026 gross emissions, carbon stock, or net greenhouse-gas balance. This is a preliminary remote-sensing screening brief, not a verified forest-carbon inventory or crediting assessment.

Spatial basis

The supplied WDPA site 124389 boundary measures 13,939.55 km² geodesically. Analyses use this polygon; the separate reported WDPA area is 13,726.25 km².

Vegetation observations

Sentinel-2 monthly NDVI means range from 0.424 to 0.481. Clear-land coverage varies substantially, and indices are not biomass or carbon-flux measurements.

Modelled uptake

MODIS GPP and PSN estimate photosynthesis. October observations end 15 October; model outputs are not emissions, removals, or net ecosystem sequestration.

Missing emissions evidence

Validated activity data, pool-specific carbon losses, and gas-specific factors are unavailable. Gross emissions remain unquantified; unknown is not zero.
