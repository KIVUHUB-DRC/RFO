What the data say

Recent findings & limits

Burned area: zero QA-valid detections Jan–Aug; Sep–Oct unavailable as of 4 Nov 2026. This is not proof of no fire.

Carbon uptake: MODIS GPP/PSN are photosynthesis model outputs. October observations end 15 Oct and have 27.8% full-month area-time coverage.

CAMS GFAS is a candidate fire-only model, but requires account access and accepted terms; no values were retrieved. It cannot represent total gross emissions.
