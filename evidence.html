What the satellite record shows

Monthly vegetation-condition observations, modelled photosynthesis estimates, and burned-area screening—shown with their observation coverage and limitations.

Sentinel-2 vegetation indices

Monthly NDVI/EVI summarize clear-land reflectance. They indicate vegetation conditions, not biomass change or carbon flux.

MODIS photosynthesis

GPP and PSN are model outputs. Spatial expansion and incomplete coverage add uncertainty; these metrics are not emissions or net ecosystem sequestration.

MODIS burned area

Zero valid detections are distinct from unavailable products. Coarse burned-area screening cannot establish that no fire occurred.

Monthly evidence register

Values are reserve-wide summaries from the processed source files. October MODIS observations cover only 1–15 October.
